Empowering communities across Zambia with waste management education

Our Mission

Marabo Waste Learning is dedicated to educating communities across Zambia about effective waste management practices. Our mission is to create a cleaner, healthier environment through accessible education and community engagement.

  • Provide accessible waste management education
  • Foster sustainable community practices
  • Reduce environmental pollution

The Challenge

With only 40% of urban waste formally collected in Zambia, we face significant environmental and public health risks due to improper waste disposal. Our platform addresses this crisis by providing interactive learning resources and fostering community action.

Improper Disposal

60% of urban waste is improperly disposed of

Health Risks

Increased disease prevalence in affected areas

Our Approach

Through multilingual e-learning modules, community engagement tools, and practical guides, we empower individuals with the knowledge and tools they need to make sustainable waste management decisions. Our content is specifically designed for the Zambian context and accessible across various technology levels.
